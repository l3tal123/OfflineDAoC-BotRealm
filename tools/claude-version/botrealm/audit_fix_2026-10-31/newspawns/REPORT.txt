HOW (judgement calls in CAPS)
Positions
- CapnBry radar sightings (sightings_index.json), region coords = zone-local + Zones.OffsetX/Y*8192 of that zone id.
  Two radar zone-id quirks found and checked against live spawns: zone 245 = the server's Hall of the Corrupt region 277
  (same local coords; median 378 units from a live same-name spawn, many within 50), and zone 0 with Z > 10000 =
  Darkness Falls 249 already in region coords (547 sightings, many exact matches with live DF spawns).
  NF zones 163-178 (region 163) are never used.
- Clusters: greedy 300-unit clusters; regular mob = one spawn per cluster at the sighting nearest the cluster centre;
  named = one spawn at the densest cluster. A named mob listed for several zones gets ONE spawn in the zone with
  positions, then period-zam habitat, then the densest cluster (never a capital city).
- NAMED = capitalised as the RADAR spelled it (the radar reads the real server's name); without radar the finding's /
  server's spelling. Realm/race first words (Albion, Hibernian, Ellyll, Tylwyth, Arawnite, Fomorian...) don't count, so
  "Albion minion" (61 sightings) and "Ellyll windchaser" are regular.
- Uthgard map dots (locations.json) only where no radar: 1 mob placed this way (lunaris primus pilus, Llyn Barfog).
Z / geometry
- Navmesh check with tools\claude-version\true165\groundz165.py (imported read-only, no bytecode written):
  radar Z kept when the navmesh floor is within 64 (525 rows); snapped to the navmesh floor when 64-200 off (17 rows,
  noted); point dropped when > 200 off or no floor within 150 (then the next sighting of the cluster is tried, up to 5);
  zones with no navmesh keep the radar Z. 103 clusters were dropped this way (mostly old Snowdonia / Pennine /
  Hadrian's Wall, where many radar Zs are 1000-4000 off the 1.65 terrain - probably later-patch geometry).

  first then region; generic species (spirit, creature, ghost, tree...) need two shared words; never for named mobs.
