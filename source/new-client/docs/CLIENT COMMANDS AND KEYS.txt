THE NEW CLIENT'S OWN COMMANDS AND KEYS (True 1.65)
==================================================

The client handles these itself; every other line or /command goes to the server (its commands are in the server's
list). Keys are the defaults of the RPG preset (Options > CONTROLS also has the client's FPS and MMO presets); every
action takes two bindings, any key, mouse button (Mouse 4 / 5, wheel) or Shift / Ctrl / Alt combination.

CLIENT COMMANDS
  /loc                  your location: zone, x, y, z and heading (as the original client)
  /follow  /stick  /face   follow / stick to / face your target
  /realmwar  /rw        the realm war map
  /craft                the tradeskill window
  /train                the trainer window, anywhere
  /qbar <bank> [bar]    switch a quickbar to a bank (bank 1-10, bar 1-3)
  /stealth  /hide       hide / come out (Stealth spec)
  /macro <name> <command>   make a macro; click a quickbar slot to place it
  /quit  /q  /logout    the server's quit (sit, countdown, character screen)

QUICKBARS
  Quickbar 1: 1 - 0      Quickbar 2: Alt+1 - 0      Quickbar 3: Ctrl+1 - 0      Shift+1 - 0: quickbar 1's bank
