1. THE VAULT IS ACCOUNT WIDE (REALM SPECIFIC) AND HOLDS COINS - CUSTOM (owner, 2026-10-10)
   1.65 had only each character's own Storage Vault, opened at a Vault Keeper NPC. It came in v1.27 (6 Oct 2001,
   Warcry patch notes): "you can store only objects in the Vaults - since money does not have weight... there is no
   need to store money in the vault". There was no account vault in 1.65: none of the 2001-2004 period sources mention
   one. The old OfflineDAoC server had both, the regular vault and a custom freeshard AccountVaultKeeper.
   Changed:
   - The vault (all 40 slots, both pages) is shared by every character of the account IN THE SAME REALM. Albion,
     Midgard and Hibernia each have their own vault. Saved in user://vaults/<account>_<realm>.json.
   - The vault holds coins: DEPOSIT / WITHDRAW at the vault window. Coins carried on a character stay that
     character's own; nothing becomes account wide automatically.
   - A character's old per-character vault items moved into the shared vault at its first login; any that found no
     room wait on the character until there is space.
   Code: game/ui/player_data.gd (_load_vault / _save_vault / vault_deposit / vault_withdraw),
         game/ui/vault_window.gd (the coin strip). Test: tests/vault_probe.gd.

TASK TARGET MARKER (2026-10-10, owner request - a QOL addition)
- The 1.65 /task system is in (see AUTONOMOUS WORK LOG / game/quests/tasks.gd). In the original, a kill task's guard
  only named a general direction ("Search to the North East and kill a ..."), and a delivery task named the receiver
  and the zone.
- Here your task's target is also marked in its own colour (violet), with the same swirling ring under NPCs and dot
  on the map as the quest markers:
  - a kill task: every live monster of that name around the spot the guard meant, plus a dot on the map; once it is
    killed, the guard to return to;
  - a delivery task: the NPC who takes the item.

AUTO LOOT (2026-10-10, owner request - a QOL option, not in 1.65)
- Like the old OfflineDAoC's /autoloot: coins and items from your kills go straight into your backpack ("You pick up
  12 copper.", "You get a small mirror and put it in your backpack.").
- On by default for every character (and for the bots when they come). Turn it off in Options > INTERFACE > LOOT >
  AUTO LOOT or with /autoloot on | off.
- With auto loot on, nothing your kills drop lies on the ground, except when your backpack is full: then that item
  stays at the monster's feet as a brown bag (as in the old OfflineDAoC: "Your backpack is full. You must drop
  something first.").
- Off: drops lie on the ground as in the original, for you to click and pick up.
- What lies on the ground: weapons and shields show their own model, everything else (armour, jewellery, coins) the
  brown bag. Items you drag out of your backpack onto the ground land there the same way ("You drop the X on the
  ground!"). Ground items vanish after 3 minutes (old server world_item_decay_time).
