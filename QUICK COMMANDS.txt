OFFLINE DAOC TRUE 1.65 v0.1 - QUICK COMMANDS AND LAUNCHER SHORTCUTS

On the launcher, click + Lv.1 under a realm to add gamebots:
  Normal click: +1 bot
  Hold Shift while clicking: +10 bots
  Hold Ctrl while clicking: +100 bots
  Bots start at level 1 and level up in the world (there is no + Lv.50 button).

COMPANIONS
Talk to (right-click) the innkeeper at your realm's traveler's inn to hire companions.
  Your first companion is free; later ones cost coin. More slots open at levels
  5, 10, 15, 20, 30, 40 and 45 (7 companions, a full group of 8).
  A companion joins at level 5 with a training weapon. Give it gear and train its specs.
Right-click your companion to open its page (gear, stats, spec, realm abilities).
Drag an item onto your companion: it wears it, or puts it in its 10-slot bag.
A dead companion is revived free by any healer of your realm (right-click the healer).
A blacksmith repairs the worn gear of the companions with you, free.

/companionui
  The hiring and companion windows send this when you click their buttons.
  You can type it or use it in a macro. Rows count from 0 (first companion = row 0).
  /companionui summon 0     calls your first companion (window open)
  /companionui dismiss 0    sends it back to the inn
  /companionui gear 0       its gear window (it must be with you)
  See ALL SERVER COMMANDS.txt for every button word.

/pull
  Orders your companions and pets to engage your selected enemy. When possible,
  a tank makes first contact before the rest of the group joins the fight.

/aggressive
  Companions attack what you attack (the default).

/defensive
  Companions stay near you and fight what comes within about 350 units, so you can
  pull mobs back to the group. Useful for harder content.

/classes
  Lists your realm's Classic and Shrouded Isles classes and their party roles.

TASKS AND QUESTS
/task
  Target a named guard (a Captain, a Sir...) for a kill task, or another
  townsperson of your realm for a delivery task. Tasks are for levels 1 to 19.
  /task with nothing targeted (or /task status) shows your current task.
  /task abort gives your task up.

/questguide
  Shows the period walkthrough of your active quests, one page at a time.
  /questguide X shows the guide of quest X. /questguide X 2 shows page 2.

/quest
  Lists your ongoing and finished quests.

/autoloot <on/off>
  Pays the coin and drops that are yours (your kills and your party's) straight
  into your bags instead of leaving them on the ground. Off by default; it saves
  one right-click per drop. Gamebots always use it, so their kills never become
  bags to walk to.

/sell all
  Target a merchant and sell everything in your five bags in one command.
  Items that merchant won't buy stay in the bags. /sell 4 sells bag 4 only,
  /sell 2-3 sells those bags, and adding nopot keeps your potions.

At level 5 a courier brings the quest "The Traveler's Inn": turn it in at the
innkeeper. At level 50 a messenger brings "The Horn of Rallying": turn it in at
the inn's Horn Handler.

THE HORN OF RALLYING (level 50)
Right-click the horn at the traveler's inn to open its window and start a great hunt
(dragon, epic dungeon, Summoner's Hall, Darkness Falls).
/horn list
  Shows the great hunts of your realm and whether each is ready.
/horn sound X
  X = the hunt's place in the list, counting from 0. Sounds the horn for it.
/horn close
  Closes the window.

TRAVEL AND TRAINING
/stables
  Lists every stablemaster horse route in your realm, one page at a time.
  /stables X shows page X.
/stables classic
  Jumps to your realm's Classic stable routes.
/stables si
  Jumps to your realm's Shrouded Isles stable routes.

/train <line> <level>
  Trains a specialization to the chosen level using your available specialization
  points. Target a trainer of your class first.

REALM EXCHANGE (beside the traveler's inn)
Right-click the broker, then click a choice (or /whisper it):
  [Browse the exchange]   [Manage my listings]   [Claim sale proceeds]

GM ONLY IN TRUE 1.65
/tele, /tc, /grind, /mobs, /spawn, /raid, /freelevel, /vault,
/buff, /xp, /rp, /hardcore, /classrog, /bountylocation, /bountyrent, /dropmr, /api,
/switch, /craftmacro, /faction, and the launcher's "Teleport to" on a bot
  A normal player gets "That command does not exist in True 1.65 Mode."
  Companions are hired at the inns instead of /spawn and /raid.

These controls apply to your hired companions, not the autonomous gamebots.
